2. Project Overview

JEEVAN-AIR (Aerial Intelligence & Rescue) is an autonomous search-and-rescue (SAR) aerial decision-support system developed by Team ZYNTAX for the Smart India Hackathon 2026 under Problem Statement SIH26177 (sponsored by Qualcomm Inc).

During major disasters (flash floods, earthquakes, landslides, industrial fires), ground rescue teams face extreme hazards and severe information deprivation. First responders frequently enter disaster zones without knowing whether victims are trapped or dead, where active fire or chemical hazards exist, or if roads have collapsed. JEEVAN-AIR addresses this operational void through an intelligent, closed-loop decision pipeline:

DETECT ⟶ VERIFY ⟶ ASSESS ⟶ PRIORITIZE ⟶ GUIDE

The system moves beyond simple optical object detection by fusing multi-spectral optical and thermal signatures, scoring victim urgency on an explainable 0–100 scale, ranking survivors into an operational extraction queue, and generating hazard-free ground ingress corridors for first responders.

3. Problem Statement

ID: SIH26177 • Title: A deployable AI-powered autonomous drone that aids search-and-rescue operations by detecting people and hazards, thereby improving responder safety and reducing victim discovery time.

Core Operational Deficiencies Solved:

  • Excessive Victim Discovery Latency: Manual foot searches in large disaster zones are perilous and slow.
  • Optical False Positives: Inanimate debris, shadows, and blankets trigger false alarms, diverting critical ground resources.
  • Lack of Urgency Prioritization: Commercial drones deliver unranked video streams, leaving commanders unable to triage which victims require immediate extraction.
  • Danger to Ground Responders: Ground personnel lack visibility into secondary hazards (advancing fire, toxic plumes) between their staging area and victim coordinates.

4. Proposed Solution

JEEVAN-AIR provides an integrated, deployable aerial command and decision-support architecture:

  • IMPLEMENTED Rescue Intelligence Engine: Standalone algorithmic core (src/services/rescueIntelligence.ts) computing multi-factor risk, triage priority, and safe foot routes.
  • IMPLEMENTED Multi-Modal AI Pipeline Abstraction: Clean interfaces for YOLOv8 person detection, radiometric LWIR thermal analysis, and spatial IoU sensor fusion (src/ai/).
  • IMPLEMENTED Tactical Ground Control Station: 11 interactive command pages presenting real-time telemetry, GIS sector maps, alert feeds, and triage cards.
  • SIMULATED Simulation Data Adapter: Mathematical simulation of drone flight kinematics, lawn-mower route stepping, and sensor incident injections (SimulationDataProvider.ts).
  • PLANNED Hardware Datalink Integration: Future MAVLink 2.0 WebSocket client (HardwareDataProvider.ts) ready for physical drone connection with zero UI rewrite.

6. Objectives

  1. Minimize Victim Discovery Latency: Accelerate aerial sector scanning through automated computer vision.
  2. Maximize Ground Responder Safety: Dynamically synthesize ground ingress paths avoiding lethal hazard buffers.
  3. Eliminate False Positives: Verify optical candidate silhouettes against biometric core body heat (35.5°C–38.2°C).
  4. Deliver Explainable Decision Support: Provide transparent, human-readable rationale for every risk score.
  5. Maintain Zero-Rewrite Hardware Readiness: Ensure the software seamlessly transitions from simulation to physical drone operations via a single configuration toggle.

13. Survivor Detection

IMPLEMENTED Abstraction • SIMULATED Frames

Combines optical silhouette detection with radiometric LWIR thermal verification:

  • Optical Detection (YoloVisionProvider.ts): Detects human silhouettes, outputting bounding box coordinates $(x, y, w, h)$ and confidence.
  • Thermal Verification (ThermalProcessingProvider.ts): Evaluates radiometric heat matrix to confirm human core temperatures ($35.5^\circ ext{C} ext{–}38.2^\circ ext{C}$).
  • IoU Sensor Fusion (MultiModalFusionEngine.ts): Correlates optical and thermal detections spatially with an IoU threshold of $0.20$. Boosts confidence on dual confirmation, discounts confidence on optical-only detections.

14. Hazard Detection

IMPLEMENTED Interface • SIMULATED Incidents

Tracks 8 operational disaster hazard categories via HazardDetectionProvider.ts:

  • Fire: Thermal combustion detection ($T > 60^\circ ext{C}$), affected radius $50 ext{m}$.
  • Flood / Flooded Area: Specular reflectance and water boundary edge indices.
  • Smoke: Atmospheric contrast degradation and aerosol dispersion.
  • Debris & Damaged Structure: Surface texture roughness and geometric collapse profiles.
  • Chemical Hazard: Plume dispersion tracking and thermal vapor contrast.

15. Rescue Intelligence Core

IMPLEMENTED Standalone Module (src/services/rescueIntelligence.ts)

Transforms raw sensing data into actionable field directives. Implements pure functional logic independent of UI frameworks, validated by 24 automated unit tests.

16. Risk Assessment

IMPLEMENTED Scoring Algorithm

Computes an explainable risk score ($0 ext{–}100$) across five weighted factors:

Evaluation Factor Weight Scoring Rules
Movement Status 0–30 pts NO_MOVEMENT / STATIC: +28 | UNKNOWN: +15 | MOVEMENT_DETECTED: +10
Estimated Condition 0–25 pts CRITICAL: +25 | UNCERTAIN: +18 | POSSIBLE: +14 | STABLE: +8
Thermal Confirmation 0–15 pts Confirmed biometric heat ($35.5^\circ ext{C} ext{–}38.2^\circ ext{C}$): +15 | Unconfirmed: +5
Hazard Proximity 0–20 pts Within buffer of Critical hazard: +20 | High hazard: +15 | Distance < 50m: +10
Terrain Accessibility 0–10 pts Flooded / impassable terrain: +10 | Debris obstruction: +5 | Clear: +2

Severity Mapping: CRITICAL (≥ 75), HIGH (50–74), MEDIUM (25–49), LOW (0–24).

17. Rescue Prioritization

IMPLEMENTED Priority Queue

Sorts survivors into an actionable queue:

  1. Filters out rejected false positives.
  2. Sorts primarily by descending riskScore.
  3. Resolves ties via sensor confidence.
  4. Assigns ordinal ranks (Priority #1, Priority #2...) rendered on dashboard badges.

18. Second-Look Verification

IMPLEMENTED Verification Workflow

Ambiguous detections (low confidence, optical-only) are placed in POSSIBLE status. Clicking REQUEST SECOND LOOK transitions the target to UNDER_REVIEW and simulates multi-spectral re-examination: confirming genuine survivors to VERIFIED (confidence elevated ≥ 88%) or rejecting false-positive debris to REJECTED.

19. Safe-Access Guidance

IMPLEMENTED A* Ingress Pathfinding

Fundamental Distinction: Drone Flight Route ≠ Ground Responder Route. The drone flies straight over rivers and fires, but ground rescue teams require safe paths on foot.

The pathfinding engine evaluates a 3×3 sector graph, marks sectors with active critical hazards as impassable obstacles, and computes the shortest hazard-free corridor from base (A1) to the victim, including walking travel time at $1.1 ext{ m/s}$.

20. Mission Replanning

IMPLEMENTED Dynamic Objective Engine

Automatically retargets the drone from broad sweep mode to localized target monitoring upon discovering critical victims or emerging fires, updating the mission banner and timeline.

28. Current Limitations

  1. Physical Drone Absent: Airframe, motors, ESCs, and battery pack are not physically constructed.
  2. Hardware Sensors Absent: Optical and thermal cameras are simulated; no physical video feeds.
  3. No Live MAVLink Datalink: Telemetry is generated via mathematical formulas in software.
  4. Coarse 3×3 Grid: Ground routing operates across 9 discrete sectors rather than continuous DEM terrain maps.
  5. Client-Side Execution: AI inference runs on the browser CPU rather than an onboard NVIDIA Jetson.

29. Future Hardware Integration

PLANNED Zero-Rewrite Hot-Swap Architecture

The system is pre-engineered for physical hardware. When the physical drone is constructed:

  1. Deploy JeevanAir Edge Bridge (Python/FastAPI) on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano connected to Pixhawk via UART.
  2. In src/hardware/config.ts, set SIMULATION_MODE = false and configure WebSocket URL.
  3. providerFactory.ts automatically switches to HardwareDataProvider. Zero UI code modified.
